Our Story

Two Decades of Flooring Excellence in Sterling Heights

Sterling Heights Carpet and Floors is the best place to call for any of your flooring needs. We have over 20 years of experience in all aspects of flooring and have built an amazing reputation in the Sterling Heights community.

We carry a huge selection of carpeting with different types of styles, colors, materials, and price ranges. We also have a great selection of other flooring such as hardwood floors, laminate floors, ceramic tile, custom vinyl tile, and many more.

Our goal is to make shopping for carpeting and flooring as easy as possible, while still offering the highest quality products, services, and installation. Most of our new customers come to us from referrals from satisfied past customers.
