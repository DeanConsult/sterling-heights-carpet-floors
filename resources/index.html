Flooring Tips & Care Guides

Vacuum Carpet Regularly

The most important thing you can do to extend the life of your carpet is vacuum regularly — at least once a week in high-traffic areas. This prevents dirt from settling deep into the fibers where it can cause premature wear and discoloration.

Clean Spills Immediately

Always blot — never rub — liquid spills on carpet or hardwood as soon as they happen. Rubbing spreads the stain and damages fibers. Use a clean white cloth and work from the outside of the spill inward. For stubborn stains, call a professional cleaner.

Use Furniture Pads on Hardwood

Place felt or rubber pads under all furniture legs to prevent scratches on hardwood floors. Even lightweight chairs can cause micro-scratches over time. Replace the pads every 12–18 months as they wear down and lose effectiveness.

Control Indoor Humidity for Hardwood

Hardwood floors expand and contract with humidity changes. Keep your home’s relative humidity between 35–55% year-round. Excessive moisture can cause warping; extremely dry conditions can cause cracking and gaps between boards.

Use Doormats at Every Entry

Placing doormats at every entry point — especially exterior doors — dramatically reduces the amount of dirt, moisture, and grit tracked onto your floors. This is one of the simplest and most effective things you can do to protect any type of flooring.

Professional Carpet Cleaning Every 12–18 Months

Even with regular vacuuming, carpets need professional deep cleaning every 12–18 months to remove embedded dirt, allergens, and bacteria. Hot-water extraction (steam cleaning) is the most effective method and is recommended by most carpet manufacturers to maintain your warranty.
