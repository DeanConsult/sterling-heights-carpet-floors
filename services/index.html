Hardwood Floors

Hardwood floors are one of the best flooring options available. They are very easy to maintain, and if properly cared for, they can last 20–30 years — all while being some of the best-looking floors out there. Sterling Heights Carpet and Floors has been installing hardwood floors since our inception. From selection to installation to care, we are with you all the way to make sure your hardwood floors come out perfect. We advise you on important factors like sun exposure and traffic patterns.

Affordable Alternative

Laminate Floors

Laminate floors are becoming increasingly popular as they are a cheaper and great-looking alternative to hardwood floors. Modern laminate is incredibly realistic in appearance and far more durable than many people expect. At your in-home appointment, our specialist can advise you on the best kind of flooring for your specific project and walk you through all available options. Laminate is a great choice for high-traffic areas and households with children or pets.

Laminate Features

  • Realistic wood & stone looks
  • Durable & scratch-resistant
  • Easy to clean and maintain
  • Great for high-traffic areas
  • Pet and child friendly
  • Budget-friendly pricing

Tile & Vinyl

Ceramic Tile & Vinyl Flooring

We are well equipped to handle your ceramic tile, custom vinyl tile, and linoleum flooring needs. Ceramic tile is an excellent option for kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways due to its durability and moisture resistance. Vinyl and linoleum offer similar benefits at a more affordable price point and come in a wide variety of styles. Our specialist can guide you through all the options and help you pick the right product for each space in your home.

  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
  • Linoleum
  • Porcelain tile
  • Mosaic & accent tile
