What We Offer

Complete Flooring Services for Sterling Heights Homes

From soft carpet to stunning hardwood, we carry everything you need. Our specialist brings it all to you — free of charge, no obligation.

Carpet Installation

Huge selection of carpet styles, colors, and materials in every price range. Loop, plush, Berber, level cut — Shaw and Mohawk brands. We bring hundreds of samples right to your living room.

Hardwood Floors

Beautiful, durable, and long-lasting. Solid and engineered hardwood in multiple species and stain colors. Our experts will advise on the best option for your traffic patterns and sun exposure.

Laminate Floors

A stunning, affordable alternative to hardwood. Modern laminate looks incredibly realistic, cleans easily, and stands up to kids and pets. Great for any room in the house.

Ceramic Tile & Vinyl

Tile and vinyl for kitchens, bathrooms, entryways, and more. Durable, moisture-resistant, and available in countless styles. We also offer luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and linoleum.

Commercial Flooring

We handle flooring for offices, warehouses, apartment complexes, and retail locations. Evening and weekend scheduling to keep your business running without interruption.

Outdoor & Specialty

Outdoor carpeting for patios, porches, and pool areas. Linoleum for utility areas and kitchens. Whatever your flooring need, we’ve seen it — and we can handle it.

Common Questions

Flooring FAQs

How does the shop-at-home service work?

Call us or submit a request online. We’ll schedule a time to visit your home with a large selection of samples — carpets, hardwood, laminate, tile, and more. Our specialist takes measurements, answers your questions, and provides a free written estimate. Evening and weekend appointments available.

How quickly can you install new floors?

For in-stock products, we offer 48-hour installation. For special orders, lead times vary by product. We’ll give you an accurate timeline at your free in-home appointment.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer 12 months interest-free financing through Synchrony Bank. Ask about financing at your in-home appointment. Subject to credit approval.

What types of flooring do you carry?

We carry carpet (loop, plush, Berber, level cut), hardwood floors, laminate floors, ceramic tile, vinyl tile, linoleum, and outdoor carpeting. We carry Shaw and Mohawk brands, among others.

Do you do commercial flooring?

Yes. We have experience installing flooring in offices, warehouses, apartment complexes, and retail locations. We work around your schedule and can accommodate evenings and weekends to minimize disruption to your business.

Where do you serve?

We are based in Sterling Heights, MI and serve the surrounding Metro Detroit area. Call us to confirm service to your area.
